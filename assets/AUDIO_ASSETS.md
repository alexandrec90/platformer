# Audio Assets List

Here is the list of recommended audio assets for the platformer. You can generate these using AI tools.

## Sound Effects (`assets/sounds/`)

1. **`jump.mp3`** (or `.wav`)
   - **Description:** Short, rising pitch sound. Used when the player jumps.
   
2. **`land.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Soft thud or impact sound. Used when the player hits the ground after a jump.

3. **`coin_pickup.mp3`**
   - **Description:** High-pitched chime, "ding", or retro arcade collection sound. Used when collecting coins.

4. **`enemy_hit.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Squish, pop, or impact sound. Used when jumping on an enemy.

5. **`player_hurt.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Grunt, oof, or retro damage noise. Used when the player takes damage.

6. **`game_over.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Short descending jingle or sad melody. Used when the player loses all lives.

## Music (`assets/music/`)

1. **`level_theme.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Upbeat, energetic, looping track. Approx 1-2 minutes long. Main background music for gameplay.

2. **`menu_theme.mp3`**
   - **Description:** Slower, ambient, or catchy track. Used for the start screen or main menu.
